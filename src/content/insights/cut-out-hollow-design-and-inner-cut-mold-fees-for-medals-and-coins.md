<h2 id="field-failure-prevention-7">5. Field Failure Prevention &amp; Pre-Shipment Inspection Protocol (Cut-Out Hollow Medal & Coin Mold Rules)</h2>

<p>Wholesale buyers hit avoidable enamel cross-bleeding, brittle rear post snaps, or dull recessed plating when artwork wall clearances drop below factory minimums before engraving.</p>

<p>Our tooling desk enforces >= 0.15mm metal walls on pins (>= 0.35mm on sculpted coins) during free 12-hour proofs and verifies XRF lead safety on every melt.</p>

<h3>Mandatory Foundry QC Checkpoints Before Bulk Release</h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Rear Post Silver Brazing Audit:</strong> High-torque soldering secures butterfly spurs above 25N pull force on <a href="/products/custom-shape-shield-challenge-coins">Custom Cut-Out Shape & Shield Challenge Coins</a> and <a href="/products/3d-zinc-alloy-keychains">Custom 3D Zinc Alloy Die-Cast Keychains</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Pantone Delta-E Metered Filling:</strong> Precision dispensers fill recessed cavities prior to 180°C kiln baking across <a href="/products/custom-spinner-race-medals">Custom Rotating Spinner Race Medals</a> and <a href="/products/bottle-opener-finisher-medals">Custom Bottle Opener Finisher Medals</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Electrophoretic Lacquer Sealing:</strong> Protective clear coats block oxidation across antique and mirror plating on <a href="/products/custom-triathlon-cycling-medals">Custom Triathlon & Cycling Heavyweight Medals</a> and <a href="/products/gold-silver-bronze-award-medals">Custom Gold Silver Bronze Antique Award Medals</a>.</li>
