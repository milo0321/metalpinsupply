<h2 id="field-failure-prevention-7">5. Field Failure Prevention &amp; Pre-Shipment Inspection Protocol: Cut-Out Hollow Medal & Coin Mold Rules</h2>

<p>Even experienced merchandisers hit avoidable quality rejections when fine artwork clearances are overlooked prior to steel die engraving. Enamel cross-bleeding, brittle rear post solder joints, and rapid antique patina tarnish account for most wholesale defect claims.</p>

<p>Before authorizing bulk production, engineers verify minimum raised metal dividing lines (&gt;= 0.15mm for pins and &gt;= 0.35mm for sculpted coins) on wet-enamel proofs. Every incoming alloy batch also clears XRF heavy-metal screening for CPSIA and REACH compliance.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Post Pull-Force &amp; Clutch Audit:</strong> High-torque silver solder joins brass spurs securely on <a href="/products/custom-shape-shield-challenge-coins">Custom Cut-Out Shape & Shield Challenge Coins</a> and <a href="/products/3d-zinc-alloy-keychains">Custom 3D Zinc Alloy Die-Cast Keychains</a>, withstanding over 25N pull force without snapping.</li>
  <li><strong>Pantone Delta-E Color Control:</strong> Syringe dispensing meters PMS pigments consistently across recessed cavities on <a href="/products/custom-spinner-race-medals">Custom Rotating Spinner Race Medals</a> and <a href="/products/bottle-opener-finisher-medals">Custom Bottle Opener Finisher Medals</a> prior to 180°C kiln baking.</li>
  <li><strong>Plating Thickness &amp; Lacquer Seal:</strong> Protective electrophoretic clear coats prevent fingerprint oxidation across mirror finishes on <a href="/products/custom-triathlon-cycling-medals">Custom Triathlon & Cycling Heavyweight Medals</a> and <a href="/products/gold-silver-bronze-award-medals">Custom Gold Silver Bronze Antique Award Medals</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Transit Drop &amp; Polybag Protection:</strong> Individual polybags, tray dividers, and 5-ply master cartons prevent plating rub during international air DDP transit.</li>
</ul>
