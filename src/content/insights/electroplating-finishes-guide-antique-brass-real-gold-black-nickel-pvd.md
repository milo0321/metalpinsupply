<p>Every batch passes six quality checkpoints between blanking and export boxing. Automated optical screening isolates surface pitting or soldering voids before attachment assembly begins.</p>

<h2 id="field-failure-prevention-4">5. Field Failure Prevention &amp; Pre-Shipment Inspection Protocol (12 Electroplating & PVD Finishes for Metal Pins)</h2>

<p>Wholesale buyers hit avoidable enamel cross-bleeding, brittle rear post snaps, or dull recessed plating when artwork wall clearances drop below factory minimums before engraving.</p>

<p>Our tooling desk enforces >= 0.15mm metal walls on pins (>= 0.35mm on sculpted coins) during free 12-hour proofs and verifies XRF lead safety on every melt.</p>
