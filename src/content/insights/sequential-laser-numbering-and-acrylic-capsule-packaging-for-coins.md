<h2 id="field-failure-prevention-20">5. Field Failure Prevention &amp; Pre-Shipment Inspection Protocol: Numbered Challenge Coins & Capsule Packaging</h2>

<p>Even experienced merchandisers hit avoidable quality rejections when fine artwork clearances are overlooked prior to steel die engraving. Enamel cross-bleeding, brittle rear post solder joints, and rapid antique patina tarnish account for most wholesale defect claims.</p>

<p>Before authorizing bulk production, engineers verify minimum raised metal dividing lines (&gt;= 0.15mm for pins and &gt;= 0.35mm for sculpted coins) on wet-enamel proofs. Every incoming alloy batch also clears XRF heavy-metal screening for CPSIA and REACH compliance.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Post Pull-Force &amp; Clutch Audit:</strong> High-torque silver solder joins brass spurs securely on <a href="/products/acrylic-coin-capsules-stands">Clear Acrylic Challenge Coin Capsules & Display Stands</a> and <a href="/products/velvet-presentation-boxes">Custom Velvet Presentation Boxes (Medals, Coins & Cufflinks)</a>, withstanding over 25N pull force without snapping.</li>
  <li><strong>Pantone Delta-E Color Control:</strong> Syringe dispensing meters PMS pigments consistently across recessed cavities on <a href="/products/firefighter-ems-challenge-coins">Custom Soft Enamel Firefighter & EMS Challenge Coins</a> and <a href="/products/antique-bronze-commemorative-coins">Custom Antique Bronze & Copper Commemorative Coins</a> prior to 180°C kiln baking.</li>
  <li><strong>Plating Thickness &amp; Lacquer Seal:</strong> Protective electrophoretic clear coats prevent fingerprint oxidation across mirror finishes on <a href="/products/diamond-cut-rope-edge-coins">Custom Diamond-Cut & Rope-Edge Challenge Coins</a> and <a href="/products/spinner-center-challenge-coins">Custom Spinner Center Challenge Coins</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Transit Drop &amp; Polybag Protection:</strong> Individual polybags, tray dividers, and 5-ply master cartons prevent plating rub during international air DDP transit.</li>
</ul>
