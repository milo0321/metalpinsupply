<h2 id="metallurgy-plating-workflow-9">3. Interactive Spinner, Slider & Hinged Pins — Substrate Metallurgy, Electroplating &amp; Workflow</h2>

<p><strong>The engineering principle is straightforward:</strong> substrate ductility governs detail sharpness. Flat two-dimensional crests stamp crisply in solid brass or iron, whereas deep sculpted forms flow cleanly in hot-chamber Zamak #3 zinc.</p>

<p>Every batch passes six quality checkpoints between blanking and export boxing. Automated optical screening isolates surface pitting or soldering voids before attachment assembly begins.</p>

  <li><strong>Die-Struck C26000 Brass &amp; Iron:</strong> Strike razor-sharp coin rims, 0.15mm borders, and flush stone-polished cloisonne on <a href="/products/custom-hard-enamel-lapel-pins">Custom Hard Enamel Lapel Pins (Cloisonne Finish)</a> and <a href="/products/custom-soft-enamel-lapel-pins">Custom Die-Struck Soft Enamel Lapel Pins</a>.</li>

<h2 id="field-failure-prevention-9">5. Field Failure Prevention &amp; Pre-Shipment Inspection Protocol (Interactive Spinner, Slider & Hinged Pins)</h2>

<p>Wholesale buyers hit avoidable enamel cross-bleeding, brittle rear post snaps, or dull recessed plating when artwork wall clearances drop below factory minimums before engraving.</p>

<p>Our tooling desk enforces >= 0.15mm metal walls on pins (>= 0.35mm on sculpted coins) during free 12-hour proofs and verifies XRF lead safety on every melt.</p>

<h3>Mandatory Foundry QC Checkpoints Before Bulk Release</h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Rear Post Silver Brazing Audit:</strong> High-torque soldering secures butterfly spurs above 25N pull force on <a href="/products/custom-spinner-race-medals">Custom Rotating Spinner Race Medals</a> and <a href="/products/sliding-charm-race-medals">Custom Sliding-Charm Interactive Race Medals</a>.</li>

  <li><strong>Electrophoretic Lacquer Sealing:</strong> Protective clear coats block oxidation across antique and mirror plating on <a href="/products/silk-screen-printed-metal-pins">Custom Silk-Screen Printed Metal Lapel Pins</a> and <a href="/products/3d-zinc-alloy-die-cast-pins">Custom 3D Zinc Alloy Die-Cast Lapel Pins</a>.</li>
