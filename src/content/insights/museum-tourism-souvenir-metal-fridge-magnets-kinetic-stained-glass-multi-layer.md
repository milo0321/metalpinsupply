<h2 id="field-failure-prevention-42">5. Field Failure Prevention &amp; Pre-Shipment Inspection Protocol: Museum & Tourism Kinetic & 3D Metal Fridge Magnets</h2>

<p>Heavy cast metal fridge magnets often suffer field returns when shallow glued magnets pop off or slide down low-ferritic brushed stainless appliance doors. Poorly recessed rear pockets and abrasive burrs also scratch luxury refrigerator finishes.</p>

<p>Before approving bulk runs, Zhongshan engineers mill 1.6mm–2.0mm deep rear pockets and lock N35–N52 magnets with UV structural epoxy before vertical shear testing on brushed steel. Every incoming alloy batch clears XRF screening for CPSIA and EN71-3 safety.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Recessed Magnet Pull &amp; Shear Audit:</strong> Vertical slip-load tests verify 18N–35N magnetic grip on brushed steel across <a href="/products/antique-brass-relief-museum-fridge-magnets">Custom Antique Brass & Bronze Relief Museum Fridge Magnets</a> and <a href="/products/glow-glitter-specialty-metal-fridge-magnets">Custom Glow-in-the-Dark & Glitter Metal Fridge Magnets</a> so heavy castings hold heavy papers securely.</li>
  <li><strong>Anti-Scratch Stainless Door Protection:</strong> Flush-recessed magnet pockets plus optional clear EVA/PET cushion pads prevent scratching across painted and bare steel finishes on <a href="/products/soft-enamel-die-struck-fridge-magnets">Custom Die-Struck Soft Enamel Metal Fridge Magnets</a> and <a href="/products/epoxy-dome-printed-metal-fridge-magnets">Custom Full-Color Offset Epoxy-Dome Metal Fridge Magnets</a>.</li>
