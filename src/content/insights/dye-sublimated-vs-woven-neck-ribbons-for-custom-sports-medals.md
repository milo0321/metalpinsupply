<h2 id="field-failure-prevention-13">5. Field Failure Prevention &amp; Pre-Shipment Inspection Protocol: Dye-Sublimated vs Woven Medal Neck Ribbons</h2>

<p>Even experienced merchandisers hit avoidable quality rejections when fine artwork clearances are overlooked prior to steel die engraving. Enamel cross-bleeding, brittle rear post solder joints, and rapid antique patina tarnish account for most wholesale defect claims.</p>

<p>Before authorizing bulk production, engineers verify minimum raised metal dividing lines (&gt;= 0.15mm for pins and &gt;= 0.35mm for sculpted coins) on wet-enamel proofs. Every incoming alloy batch also clears XRF heavy-metal screening for CPSIA and REACH compliance.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Post Pull-Force &amp; Clutch Audit:</strong> High-torque silver solder joins brass spurs securely on <a href="/products/military-drape-ribbon-medals">Custom Military & Veteran Drape Ribbon Medals</a> and <a href="/products/custom-martial-arts-bjj-medals">Custom Martial Arts, Karate & Jiu-Jitsu Medals</a>, withstanding over 25N pull force without snapping.</li>
  <li><strong>Pantone Delta-E Color Control:</strong> Syringe dispensing meters PMS pigments consistently across recessed cavities on <a href="/products/bottle-opener-finisher-medals">Custom Bottle Opener Finisher Medals</a> and <a href="/products/magnetic-interlocking-race-medals">Custom Magnetic Interlocking Race Series Medals</a> prior to 180°C kiln baking.</li>
