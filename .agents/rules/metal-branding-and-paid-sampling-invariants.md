## 2. Core Manufacturing Craft Matrix (Pins, Medals, Coins & Metalware)
- **Cloisonne / Pro-Enamel (Hard Enamel)**: Stamped iron/brass filled with synthetic enamel paste, fired, and stone-polished completely flush with raised metal borders (`+$0.15–$0.25/pc` over soft enamel).
- **Die-Struck Soft Enamel**: Stamped recessed fields filled with Pantone-matched enamel baked at 180°C so raised metal borders sit tactilely higher than color wells (optional clear epoxy dome).
- **Photo-Etched Brass**: 0.8mm–1.0mm chemically etched brass for intricate fine lines, lightweight badges, and cut-out filigree bookmarks.
- **Offset CMYK & Silk-Screen Printing**: Full-color photographic gradients sealed under clear protective epoxy dome on flat brass/stainless blanks.
- **3D Zinc Alloy Die-Casting**: Molten Zamak #3 injected into CNC-sculpted steel molds for high-relief multi-level medallions, cut-outs, and spinner assemblies.
